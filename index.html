I strive in giving impactful experiences, to explore critical questions and push boundaries.

With a Master in Digital Games and a Bachelor in Digital Society, I sit between two worlds: Where I build digital experiences as an opportunity to study how digitalization reshapes culture, social structures and daily life.

With my fellow students, I designed a rhythm-zombie shooter (Unity, 88/100), tested game elements for Mighty Boards, and most recently designed XR trainings for the EHS industry at Altheria Solutions. I care about playtest data, lucid systems, and experiences that mean something to the "enjoyer". I belive that games are impactful tools with infinite potential.
